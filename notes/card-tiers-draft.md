# Card tiers: the new list (draft for checking)

Nothing in the game has changed yet. Finished cards stay finished whatever happens.

**How it's worked out:** a rating for every player, then ranked *within his position*, so every tier has keepers, defenders, midfielders and forwards in the same share. The rating adds up:
- how long he played (with diminishing returns: 400 games isn't twice as good as 200)
- what he produced *per game* for his position (goals and assists for forwards and midfielders, clean sheets for keepers)
- PL titles and seasons in a top-four side (counted less for squad players who didn't play much)
- Champions League, World Cup, Golden Boots, and Player of the Year awards (PFA and FWA)

**💎 Icon** (new, 7 pieces): the Premier League Hall of Fame, plus the six best-rated players not in it yet.
