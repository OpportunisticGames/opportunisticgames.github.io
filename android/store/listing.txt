FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
How well do you really know English top-flight football since 1992?

Spin the reels and build an XI from over 5,000 real footballers. Can you find the goal machines among the journeymen? Every player, his clubs and his career are in there.

⚽ ULTIMATE WILDCARD: build the XI with the most goals, assists or appearances, with wildcards to play
🌪️ CHAOS: appoint a manager, fill the CHAOS meter and survive tornadoes, VAR, alien abductions and last-minute madness
🃏 HAT-TRICK: a football card game for four. Bid, win tricks and outplay the computers or your mates
🎯 TARGETS: hit the number exactly, take on the Treble or chase a Mystery Target
📅 DAILY GAMES: the Daily Ultimate, Footle (guess the mystery player in 8), the Daily Club Grid, Daily CHAOS and more, with streaks
🌐 PLAY ONLINE: race your mates in a Live Race, take turns in a Draft Duel, or tap Quick match for a new game every day
🦘 More to play: Club Hopper, Higher or Lower, Who Am I?, Guess the Tally and Moneyball
📒 Collect players in your album, build your Dream XI and unlock badges
🏟️ Pick your club for club colours, a daily Club Footle and a Club XI draft
🔔 Notifications when it's your move, and light and dark themes

No ads. No tracking. Just football.

Goal Machine is a free, fan-made game by Opportunistic Games. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any league, club or player. Player statistics are compiled from public sources.
